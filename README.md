## 🛠️ Troubleshooting: Quick Fixes for Common Issues

Even the best software sometimes needs a little help. Here are solutions to the most common problems users face:

### ❌ "SpeechLens Won't Open"

- **Check your Mac's privacy settings:** Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. Scroll down and look for a message about SpeechLens being blocked. Click **"Allow Anyway."**
- **Restart your Mac:** Sometimes a fresh start clears up temporary hiccups.

### ❌ "The Microphone Doesn't Work"

- **Grant microphone permission:** Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone**. Make sure SpeechLens is checked ON.
- **Check your input device:** In SpeechLens, make sure the correct microphone is selected in the dropdown menu.
- **Test with QuickTime:** Open QuickTime, start a new recording, and see if your built-in mic works. If it doesn't, check your Mac's sound settings.

### ❌ "The Sound Is Robotic or Distorted"

- **Lower the enhancement strength:** Switch from High to Medium or Low in the settings.
- **Make sure you're using a good mic:** Built-in mics can be quieter. Try a USB headset if possible.
- **Update macOS:** Apple regularly improves audio drivers. Go to **System Settings → General → Software Update**.

### ❌ "The App Is Slow"

- **Close other heavy apps:** Games and video editors can slow things down.
- **Check your memory:** If your Mac has 8 GB RAM, close browser tabs you're not using.
- **Update to the latest version:** New updates often come with speed improvements.
